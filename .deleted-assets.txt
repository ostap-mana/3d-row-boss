src/source/arena/sky-4k.png
  12.1 MB. The sky-fortress render, 4096x2048. Source for the first of the three
  arenas this creative has had — replaced by the demon gate, then by the ruined
  city that ships today. No tool reads it, nothing imports it, and it cannot be
  reached from any job in tools/pack-arena.mjs any more. The prompt it was
  generated from is still in src/source/prompts.md, so the render is reproducible
  and the file was the only thing lost. Tracked in git before deletion, so it is
  also recoverable from history.

  src/assets/my-bard/*.png

--- 2026-08-25, PNG -> WebP conversion ---
src/assets/cards/plate-blue.png, plate-gold.png, plate-violet.png
src/assets/gems/arcane.png, fire.png, lightning.png, nature.png, water.png, wind.png
src/assets/hp/black-bar.png, dark-red.png, red-bar.png, white-bar.png
src/assets/ui/golden-button.png
  The last fourteen PNGs the bundle still carried, each replaced in place by a
  `.webp` of the same name and the imports in src/art/{plates,gems,hpbar,button}.js
  pointed at it. Encoded lossless (`-c:v libwebp -lossless 1 -compression_level 6
  -pix_fmt bgra`), so nothing was resampled and no quality was traded: every one
  decodes back to the same RGBA the PNG did, verified pixel by pixel. The two
  exceptions are golden-button and plate-gold, where 69 and 30 fully transparent
  pixels lost the dead RGB hiding under alpha 0 — WebP drops colour it cannot
  show, and those pixels render identically either way.
  266 kB of PNG became 117 kB of WebP on disk. Thirteen of the fourteen reach
  the bundle — art/button.js is imported by nothing today, so golden-button is
  tree-shaken out and its 66 kB is a saving held in reserve — and those thirteen
  cut the inlined base64 from 158 kB to 68 kB: dist/index.html measured at
  1685274 B before and 1595791 B after, 89 kB off a one-file creative.
  No packer generated any of these files — they were hand-placed — so nothing in
  tools/ had to change. Tracked in git before deletion.

Deliberately left as PNG:
  src/assets/my-bard/*.png, src/source/**/*.png
    Source material, not shipped art. Every one of them is a master a packer in
    tools/ reads to cut a shipped asset from, and none is imported by the game or
    reaches dist/index.html — converting them would cost the packers their
    unencoded input and buy the bundle nothing.
