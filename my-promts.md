## Motion for the video (Wan 2.2 i2v)

Do NOT feed the finished card into i2v — the model will melt the VICTORY
letters and the PLAY NOW text. Render her ALONE on a flat background, animate
that, then composite the clip over the static UI plate.

Each block is one shot. Keep one motion per shot; two fight each other.

### 1. The toast — the safe one
```
She snaps the golden goblet from her chest up over her head in one sharp move.
The glowing green liquid sloshes over the rim and trails sparks. Her torn
crimson mantle lifts behind her on the follow-through and settles. Her smirk
widens, eyes locked on camera. Slow push-in. Embers drift upward through frame.
```

### 2. The mantle gust — the poster shot
```
She plants her fist on her hip and holds still. A hard gust hits her from
behind: the torn crimson mantle snaps open wide, the frayed hem cracks like a
flag, loose strands of blue-black hair whip across her face. She does not move
her feet. Static camera, faint handheld drift. Embers streak past the lens.
```

### 3. The blade slam — the heavy one
```
She drives the crimson blade down into the ground. On impact a ring of orange
sparks bursts outward along the floor and the dust jumps. Her mantle and hair
blow upward from the shockwave, then fall. Low angle, camera shakes once on the
hit, then pushes in slowly.
```

### 4. The turn — the reveal
```
She stands with her back three-quarters to camera, then turns her head and
shoulders around toward the lens. Her mantle swings across the frame with the
turn. The smirk arrives last, on the final frames. Camera orbits slowly to the
left against her turn. Crimson rim light sweeps across her face as she comes
around.
```

### 5. The goblet to camera — the CTA beat
```
She thrusts the goblet forward toward the lens in a fast salute. Green liquid
and sparks fly past the camera and blur. She holds the pose, head tilted,
grinning. Camera pulls back from the goblet to reveal her full stance. Fast
start, hard stop.
```

Negative for every shot:
```
morphing face, changing hairstyle, extra arms, extra fingers, wings, text,
watermark, warped armour, character drifting out of frame, cuts, scene change
```
