1. Sometimes the element mix timing is too fast/random. As a player, I don't really feel when the next mix is coming. It goes like: I've just found an element, I swipe - and right at that moment a mix starts, then another one right after, then a third one, and my screen is blocked, I can't do anything. It reads as half-glitch, half-unreadable mechanic - and this mechanic should ideally work on a subconscious level. My best result was 1% of the boss's HP. Or maybe I even won once - but the whole process is so unreadable, and there was definitely no victory screen, so I'm not sure.

2. Connected to the above point: that's all with me having the ability to replay and "learn" how to play it. For guys who get one session only, it will be a fast mess with a redirect to the store - and they won't be happy about it. Even recalling my own first session - it was just chaos. I understood I need to line the elements up in a row... actually, it might not even be clear that you need to line them up at all. Maybe you can add some mini-instruction: show an element moving and becoming the third in a row, so the mechanic is explained to the player visually. I knew it was Match 3 only from our discussions, and I'm not sure I would've caught it on the fly that fast. If we leave it as is, I think we can trigger negative feelings: "they gave me a game, I didn't understand it, everything was super fast, and then I got redirected to the store".

3. I can feel the boss attacking me, but I can't really see it. The feeling part is actually done well (sounds, screen shake, the damage numbers - all of it creates the sense that something bad is happening, that I'm taking damage). But visually I don't see it, maybe bc of my heroes are right under my fingers at the bottom of the screen and i don't see them. It also might be caused by too many of them on screen at once.

4. The boss doesn't stand out from the background. Background is very bright and it reads like a background for good characters, not for a boss. And the boss himself can look much cooler: very evil, scary, powerful - a cool angry AI generation, or even a simple repeatable "monster scratching" attack animation for the moments when the matching time runs out and the shake starts. He could roar or something. There's definitely room to improve here.

5. Sound was confusing. In some sessions it was there, and then several dozen sessions had no sound at all. I was clicking the iPhone volume buttons up and down, but it turned out that most likely a plain single click enables the sound - while dragging the elements doesn't. And dragging is the only finger mechanic in Match 3 - nobody clicks there. That's a problem, because the sound is cool and adds a lot of feeling - and half of my games were without it.

Overall: on the level of feelings, I think this is great and looks almost finished but there are still some improvements you can play with and they always will be there even after delivery, it's normal.
